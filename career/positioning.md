# Positioning — Proof Points Bank

Measurable results with clear ownership. **No vague responsibilities, no buzzwords.** If a bullet can't answer "what changed, by how much, because of what I did," it doesn't belong here.

## Proof points

### Team / org health
- **Team NPS +12.2** — sustained improvement under my leadership
- **Transactional NPS +5.6** — client-facing service quality lift
- **93.3% OE satisfaction** — Operational Excellence program

### Product engagement
- **3x Decision Support engagement** — attributed to product changes I led

### Revenue / risk
- **~$1M revenue protected** — via Savvion workflow automation

### Scale of ownership
- **77+ enterprise clients** — under direct account/program ownership
- **Built Product Enablement function from scratch** — org design, hiring, ops cadence, measurement

## Usage rules
- Match the proof point to the role's stated priorities before including
- Never combine two metrics into one bullet — one claim per line, one owner, one outcome
- Refresh numbers here in place when they change; never fork this file
