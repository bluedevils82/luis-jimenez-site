# Inbox

Zero-friction capture. Drop anything here — links, half-thoughts, screenshots, meeting notes, voice-memo transcripts. No organizing rules apply.

Everything here is unprocessed. It gets routed out when it earns a home in `projects/`, `career/`, or gets deleted. Nothing gets promoted straight to `wiki/`.

## Contents
- Loose notes and captures pending triage
- `_migration-log.md` — audit trail for the initial notes migration (created when step 3 runs)
